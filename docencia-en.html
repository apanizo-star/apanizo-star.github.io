Teaching

Teaching

University teaching and specialized training in Indigenous languages, lexicography, language policy and planning, interculturality, writing, and editing.

My teaching focuses primarily on Indigenous languages, lexicography, language policy and planning, intercultural bilingual education, interculturality, academic writing, and editing. I have taught in undergraduate and master's programs, diploma programs, and specialized training courses in Peru and in international programs.

Indigenous languages, Quechua, and Amerindian linguistics

Since 2022, I have taught Introduction to Quechua at Universidad de Ingeniería y Tecnología (UTEC). Since 2025, I have taught Amerindian Linguistics Workshops I and II in the Master's Program in Intercultural Bilingual Education at Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia. I have also participated in the training of Indigenous-language interpreters and translators for Peru's Ministry of Culture.

Language policy and planning

At the Graduate School of Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú, I teach the Seminar on Language Policy. In 2025, I taught the module Planning, implementation, and evaluation of language policies in the Diploma Program on Policy and Planning for the Cultural and Linguistic Revitalization of Indigenous Societies in Latin America and the Caribbean, organized by FILAC, FUNPROEIB Andes, and the Ibero-American Institute of Indigenous Languages.

Lexicography and lexicology

I have taught Lexicography and Lexicology in graduate programs, including the Master's Program in Andean Linguistics and Education at Universidad Nacional del Altiplano. At PUCP, I have taught the Seminar on Lexicology and Introduction to Lexicography.

Interculturality and education

Since 2022, I have taught the module Citizenship and Interculturality in the Diploma Program in Languages and Cultures at Universidad Mayor de San Simón and FUNPROEIB Andes, a program aimed at Bolivian Indigenous students and at processes of cultural and linguistic revitalization.

Writing, editing, and communication

My teaching experience also includes courses in writing, written communication, and lexical-semantic analysis. At Escuela de Edición de Lima, I taught Lexical-Semantic Analysis within the Comprehensive Style-Correction Program from 2020 to 2023. At PUCP, I have taught writing and communication courses, and at UTEC I also teach Communication Laboratory 2.
