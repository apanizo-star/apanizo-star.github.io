Projects

Projects and areas of work

Fields of work in linguistics, lexicography, language policy, editing, and public outreach.

Lexicography and language resources

I design, develop, and review dictionaries and other lexicographic resources, both print and digital, with particular attention to Peru's Indigenous languages. My work in this field encompasses linguistic, editorial, and technological dimensions, from defining macrostructure and microstructure to designing platforms for dictionary building and consultation.

Dictionary of the Yine language

I directed the design and development of a digital dictionary of the Yine language as a resource for consultation and language documentation. The project forms part of my experience in creating lexicographic tools for speakers, researchers, and other users.

Language policy and planning

From 2015 to 2020, I headed the Directorate of Indigenous Languages at Peru's Ministry of Culture. During that period, I contributed to the development of the public-policy framework for Indigenous languages and language rights and to the implementation of language documentation and revitalization initiatives.

Language revitalization and documentation

I develop and advise initiatives aimed at strengthening, documenting, and revitalizing Indigenous languages, with a particular interest in the relationship between public policy, community participation, and the production of language resources.

Open technologies and Wikimedia

I explore the use of open technologies and the Wikimedia ecosystem for creating, preserving, and disseminating resources in Indigenous languages. I am particularly interested in their application to dictionaries, lexicographic repositories, educational content, and language documentation.

Editing and content production

I edit, review, and prepare academic publications, educational materials, technical documents, and outreach articles. This work includes linguistic correction as well as the organization, coherence, and editorial adaptation of content. I also provide editorial support for literary writing projects.

Surf, maritime culture, and public outreach

As a surfer, I have written and edited on surf since 1994. My work in this area addresses the history and culture of Peruvian surfing, its communities and emblematic places, maritime heritage, and the conservation of surf breaks and the coastline.
