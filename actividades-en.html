A career of outreach and public debate

I have participated as a speaker, lecturer, workshop facilitator, and moderator in academic and professional activities related to Indigenous languages, language rights, revitalization, lexicography, interculturality, and public policy. This page presents a selection of those activities.

Other topics addressed
